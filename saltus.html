Healthcare

Saltus

A visit-preparation view, generated from a patient's own record.

The problem

Patients managing an ongoing condition can fall through gaps between visits — something ordered or changed that never gets confirmed as closed, with nobody actively watching to make sure it does.

What I built

A prototype that reviews a patient's record between visits and flags what might need a second look before the next appointment — built to support clinical review, not replace it.

For: family physicians who want to know what's changed since a patient's last visit before they walk in, not reconstruct it from scratch.

Key takeaway: supports clinical review — it doesn't diagnose or recommend treatment, and every flag traces back to the record.
